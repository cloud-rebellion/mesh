# Illustrative review inbox: product requirements document

## Summary

Fictional review-inbox example demonstrating this proposed layout. It records proposed behavior and honest gaps, not production facts or verification.

## Problem and audience

Illustrative scenario: reviewers need to distinguish items awaiting their review from items already completed. This is a fictional example, not a verified user finding.

## Outcomes and success measures

Proposed outcome: a reviewer can identify pending work and confirm when a review is complete. No measured baseline or numerical target is supplied.

## Scope and boundaries

This example covers a review inbox and marking a review complete. Assignments and automated reminders are outside this example's scope.

## Product requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| PR-001 | Show the signed-in reviewer's pending items. | Not assigned |
| PR-002 | Let the reviewer mark an eligible item complete. | Not assigned |

## Dependencies and constraints

Authentication and authorization are dependencies. The identity provider and assignment policy remain undecided in this fictional scenario.

## Acceptance criteria

Planned check for PR-001: a reviewer sees their eligible pending items. Planned check for PR-002: a completed item leaves the pending list. Neither check has been performed.

## Risks and open questions

Who determines eligibility? Can completion be reversed? Owners and review dates are not assigned.
